\section{Motivation and Aim}
\label{sec::MotivationAndAim}

The motivation behind this research stems from the growing need for robots to operate effectively in dynamic human environments. Traditional behaviour-based or learning-based robotic systems often fall short in adapting to complex social interactions and changing contexts. There is a pressing demand for robots that can not only perform tasks autonomously but also engage with humans in a socially aware and contextually appropriate manner.
