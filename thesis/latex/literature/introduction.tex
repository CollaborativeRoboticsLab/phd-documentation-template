\chapter{Literature Review}
\label{ch::LiteratureReview}

This chapter provides a comprehensive overview of the literature, establishing the background for the research.

Section \ref{sec::LiteratureReview::RobotControlHRI} contains a comprehensive literature review on the Robotic systems that can be used for HRI. First, the literature on behaviour-based systems is discussed in Subsection \ref{sec::LiteratureReview::RobotControlBBM}. Then, literature about learning-based systems is addressed in Subsection \ref{sec::LiteratureReview::RobotControlLBM}. Then, generative systems that utilise Large Language Models are discussed in Subsection \ref{sec::LiteratureReview::RobotControlLLM}. Subsection \ref{sec::LiteratureReview::RobotControlVLM} includes literature on Vision Language Models and how generative robotic systems work with them. Following that, Vision Language Action models are discussed in Subsection \ref{sec::LiteratureReview::RobotControlVLA} and Vision Language Navigation models are discussed in Subsection \ref{sec::LiteratureReview::RobotControlVLN}.

Then, long-horizon task planning is discussed in the Section \ref{sec::LiteratureReview::LongHTP}.

Finally, literature on Social aware navigation is discussed in the Section \ref{sec::LiteratureReview::SAN}
