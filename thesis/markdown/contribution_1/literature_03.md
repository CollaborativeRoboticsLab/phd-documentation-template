# Behaviour-Based Control Architectures in Robotics

Behaviour representations such as FSMs, HFSMs, and BTs sit within a broader question of robotic control architecture: how a robot senses the environment, selects actions, and coordinates behaviour over time. At this higher level, an orchestrator can be evaluated in terms of reactivity, that is, how quickly it can switch behaviour in response to changing conditions, and modularity, that is, how cleanly its behaviour can be decomposed into reusable building blocks. These two criteria are especially important for autonomous systems operating in uncertain environments, because the controller must both respond promptly and remain manageable as the number of behaviours grows.
