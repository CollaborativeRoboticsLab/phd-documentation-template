## Motivation and Aim

This research seeks to revolutionise socially aware robotics by shifting from a passive, behaviour-based or learning-based robotic operation to an active, interaction-driven paradigm. As robotic applications continue to expand across various sectors, including healthcare, logistics, and the service industry, the ability of robots to engage meaningfully with human counterparts becomes increasingly crucial. The study aims to bridge this gap by introducing an advanced behaviour-based planning system that enables robots to navigate and interact within dynamic human environments in a socially intelligent manner.

To achieve this, the research proposes a novel, behaviour-based planning system meticulously designed to integrate foundational models for enhanced adaptability and responsiveness. By leveraging this system, robots can develop a deeper contextual awareness, allowing them to predict, interpret, and respond to human behaviours in a way that aligns with social norms and expectations.

Ultimately, by providing theoretical insights and practical tools, this research seeks to inspire and empower fellow researchers to embrace, refine, and advance the proposed framework. The findings will contribute to the broader field of human-robot interaction (HRI), fostering innovation in developing autonomous systems that can coexist and collaborate with humans in a socially intelligent and intuitive manner.
