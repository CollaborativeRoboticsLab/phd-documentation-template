## Research Problem

Robotic systems have gradually evolved over several decades to achieve the versatile control and planning systems available today. From Reactive systems and Deliberate systems to their combined version of hybrid systems and, finally, more abstracted behaviour-based systems, evolution has layered each new system's architecture on top of the previous generation while making the latest generation comparatively abstract. Deviating slightly from the evolution, learning-based systems encompass two categories: *Decoupled learning-based systems*, which build upon previous generations, and *Coupled learning-based systems*, which implement the entire planning and control pipeline independently and are entirely self-contained, without reliance on prior generations.
