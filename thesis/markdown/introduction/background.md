## Background

Day by day, robotics gradually becomes an integral part of our lives. Robotics has been integrated into various sectors, including logistics, manufacturing, and healthcare. Although robots were initially limited to factories and surrounded by barriers, human interaction with them has increased. In sectors such as healthcare, a rapid increase in robots designed to coexist with humans can be seen. New research areas, such as Human-Robot Interaction (HRI), and new concepts, like socially aware robotics, have gained prominence.
