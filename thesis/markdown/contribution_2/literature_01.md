# Fault Tolerance in Robotic Systems

Fault tolerance in robotic systems is best understood within the broader language of dependability. Robustness, fault tolerance, resilience, reliability, and dependability are related terms, but they describe different ways in which a robotic system can continue to provide trustworthy service. Robustness is primarily absorptive: it describes the ability of hardware and control loops to withstand bounded disturbances without changing structure, for example through sealed enclosures, conservative actuator sizing, or feedback controllers with sufficient gain margins. Reliability is narrower and more statistical, focusing on the ability of components or subsystems to perform as required over time under stated conditions. Dependability is the broader system-level goal, referring to service that can justifiably be trusted.
